How to read this

A YES price of 0.62 means the market pays $1 per YES share if the event happens, and the last buyer paid about 62¢ — an implied 62% probability. Thin markets move a lot on small fills, so OddsMind shows the fill depth and how concentrated the flow is before you trust the number.
